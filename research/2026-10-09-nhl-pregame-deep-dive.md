## What the Play'Em STR8 positional chart contributes

The supplied screenshots rank potential scorers based on goals conceded per game to their *position*. We now independently collect **opponent full-game goals allowed to C, LW, RW, D** from official completed-game NHL boxscores. Corsi/Fenwick are separately derived from official NHL **5v5** play-by-play. Do NOT conflate them:
- **Goals allowed by position / game** = goals actually scored by *all* opposition players of that position divided by team games sampled, not individual player scoring success probability.
- **Team Corsi/Fenwick** = possession shot attempt shares only at 5v5; neither is a validated expected-goals projection.
- **H2H** means this named player vs that opponent's actual recorded prior games and today's **exact prop threshold**.
- This October early-season sample is extremely small (2–3 observed current-season games in some matchups). Never label an opponent "elite target" or assign weights until a meaningful current-season sample supports it. Different charts and date windows need not match. Sample denominators and source are always displayed.

## Engineering implementation and limits

- \`lib/nhl-position-allowance.js\`: verifies official NHL final boxscore scorer C/LW/RW/D goals, original scoreboard totals, excludes unfinished matches and scores the *opponent* GA by position. Sources are captured in each published scorer's matchup table.
- \`lib/nhl-possession.js\`: derives separate 5v5 Corsi/Fenwick, shot environment and descriptive PDO with blocked-shot attribution corrected.
- \`lib/free-nhl.js\`: joins verified positional and possession context, L5/L10 logs, real opponent H2H, TOI and manual injury reports; no fake xG, RAPM or lineups.
- \`index.html\`: simple WHY THIS PICK reasons, L5/L10 and H2H summaries, one-tap historical bar chart, collapsible position scoring table. Denominator visible.
- \`scripts/publish-board.js\`: data-only GitHub Releases publishing, compact scorer GA tables and original picks so no Vercel build needed.
- No market odds or full SGP combined price from bet365/FanDuel/DraftKings are independently verified. Never invent them or imply a win probability.
- At 4 PM EDT the planned pregame refresh should re-publish and record a frozen original pick slate. Next-day grading learns only from that real frozen slate.
